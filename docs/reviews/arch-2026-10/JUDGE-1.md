## 3. Waiver policy for C5 (declared now, so it cannot bend later)

A function over 100 lines or gocognit 30 is **waived** (does not count toward the ≤ 3) when it is a tokenizer, a decoder/validator of one wire format, or a single type-mapping switch, **and** has a table-driven test of its own. ## 4. Ruling on the two behaviour bugs

Both are **real, verified, and should be fixed now as standalone bug-fix PRs, before slice 1**, each with its own Go test and a Rodney check. Reasons: they are small (S each); fixing them first keeps every cleanup slice behaviour-preserving (the surveyor had to mark slices 5 and 8 as "behaviour" only because of these); and both fixes delete code the cleanup would otherwise have to move.

- **F2.** Go owns the comparison. Either (a) return the definition's fingerprint in `ConnectionDetail` and add a binding that computes the draft's fingerprint, or (b) make `workspace.Fingerprint` honour `FingerprintFields`. I rule for **(a)**: the Go comment ("erring towards inclusion is the safe side") is the right trust posture, and `FingerprintFields` then becomes a UI hint only ("these fields re-bind the sign-in") or is deleted. Delete `fingerprintOf` and the hard-coded fallback list in TS. Test: changing `region` or a non-`sslmode` option makes `bindingChanged` true.
- **F3.** `SkillsView` sends `rev` (fetch it when the banner is raised, as `QueryEditorView.vue:57` does), and `resolveSkillConflict` passes `req.Rev` (when non-empty) instead of re-reading `cur.Rev`, matching the query/notebook branches. Test: a skill changed on disk twice between banner and "Keep mine" is a conflict, not an overwrite.
