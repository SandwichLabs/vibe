Restart to open Northwind Client?

Northwind Client signs in to MotherDuck with a different login than Acme Analytics. Vibe can only use one MotherDuck login at a time, so it needs to restart to switch.

Your notebooks are saved first, and Vibe will reopen on Northwind Client by itself.
