# Vendored fonts

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and are
bundled so Vibe Studio renders identically offline (local-first: no CDN).

| Family | Files | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fraunces (variable: opsz, wght) | Fraunces-normal-latin*.woff2 | https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces |
| Inter (variable: wght) | Inter-normal-latin*.woff2 | https://github.com/rsms/inter |
| JetBrains Mono (variable: wght) | JetBrainsMono-normal-latin*.woff2 | https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono |

The licence texts are the `OFL-<Family>.txt` files beside the fonts; they are also reproduced in
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md (generated by `task notices`).

Subsets: latin and latin-ext, as served by Google Fonts (2026-09).
